Project Introduction

Transforming Traditional Poultry into Precision Smart Husbandry

The poultry industry represents one of the fastest-growing agricultural sectors worldwide, providing vital nutrition and livelihoods. However, traditional poultry farm operations in developing rural and semi-urban areas are plagued by manual record-keeping, delayed diagnosis of flock epidemics, and unpredictable microclimate fluctuations.

Sudden spikes in ambient temperature above 34°C cause severe heat stress and spike mortality. Undetected build-ups of toxic ammonia gas ($NH_3 \ge 25\text{ ppm}$) cause acute respiratory distress, while feed and water line failures go unnoticed until significant growth retardation occurs.

The Smart Poultry Farm Management and Monitoring System solves this via continuous IoT telemetry, predictive AI health risk modeling, automated Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) tracking, and native bilingual vernacular support (English and Telugu) for grassroots adoption.

Engineering Goals

Project Objectives

Engineered to achieve quantifiable biological, operational, and financial advancements.

1. Continuous IoT Telemetry

Deploy real-time environmental sensors inside poultry sheds to continuously record temperature, relative humidity, and hazardous ammonia ($NH_3$) levels, generating automated threshold alarms within 1 second of abnormal readings.

Target: Sub-second alert latency

2. AI Disease Risk Diagnostics

Implement machine learning algorithms and clinical diagnostic heuristics that correlate flock age, feed decline, environmental stress, and clinical symptom keywords to predict high-risk avian diseases before mass outbreak.

Target: Multi-factor risk scoring

3. Precision FCR Optimization

Automate feed and weight logging to compute accurate Feed Conversion Ratio ($\text{FCR} = \frac{\text{Feed Intake}}{\text{Live Weight Gain}}$) against standard breed curves (Cobb 500, Ross 308) to eliminate costly feed wastage.

Target: 1.5 - 1.8 benchmark FCR

4. Complete Lifecycle Traceability

Provide end-to-end auditability across the entire farm lifecycle: batch arrival, daily egg production, damaged egg logging, mortality reconciliation, vaccine dosage schedules, and flock sale ledger.

Target: Zero unrecorded bird variance

5. Role-Based Ecosystem

Design a secure multi-tenant architecture with dedicated interfaces for Farmers (operations & telemetry), Veterinarians (remote clinical prescriptions & symptom reviews), and Admins (macro-analytics & audit).

Target: Role-based data firewall

6. Grassroots Accessibility

Eliminate digital barriers for non-English speaking agrarian workers through instantaneous bilingual localization (English + Telugu) and a lightweight mobile smartphone PWA interface with offline resilience.

Target: 100% vernacular usability

System Modules

Comprehensive suite of 16 integrated modules managing every aspect of poultry operations.

Module 01

Dashboard

Real-time KPI summary: live bird count, today's feed, water, egg collection, mortality, live IoT gauge, and one-click alert dismissals.

Module 02

Farm Management

Multi-farm and multi-shed configuration with geolocations, maximum capacity, farm type (Broiler / Layer / Country), and biosecurity settings.

Module 03

Flock Management

Batch creation with Batch ID, breed selection (Cobb 500, Ross 308, Country), initial chicks count, arrival date, target harvest weight, and status.

Module 04

Feed Management

Daily feed consumption logging, feed type (Starter, Grower, Finisher), feed hopper level telemetry, and automatic 7-day feed requirement forecasting.

Module 05

Water Management

Daily water meter intake tracking in liters, water tank ultrasonic level telemetry, and automatic low-reservoir alerts ($\le 20\%$).

Module 06

Egg Production

Layer flock egg collection counters, damaged / cracked egg reconciliation, daily laying percentage calculation, and crate packaging records.

Module 07

Mortality Tracking

Daily bird casualty logging, cause-of-death tags, cumulative mortality percentage tracking, and automated alerts upon sudden mortality spikes.

Module 08

Weight Monitoring

Sample bird weighing entries, average flock weight calculation, growth curves comparison against Cobb/Ross standard, and market harvest ready notification.

Module 09

Temperature Monitoring

Continuous shed temperature measurement via DHT22 sensors, heat-stress threshold triggers ($T \ge 34^\circ\text{C}$), and cooling pad activation alerts.

Module 10

Humidity Monitoring

Relative humidity percentage tracking to maintain optimal shed comfort (50%–70%), prevent wet litter, and control coccidial oocyst sporulation.

Module 11

Vaccination & Medicine

Vaccination schedule based on bird age (Ranikhet, Lasota, IBD, Fowl Pox), administration routes, veterinarian prescriptions, and completion logs.

Module 12

Expenses & Sales

Financial ledger tracking chick purchase, feed bags, medicines, labor, electricity, flock sales, and egg sales with automated profit/loss computation.

Module 13

Inventory

Real-time tracking of feed bags in store, medicine vials, disinfectant stock, spare drinker nipples, and automatic re-order threshold warnings.

Module 14

Tasks & Notifications

Daily shed chores schedule (cleaning, feeding, litter turning, vaccination), overdue alerts, multi-channel SMS delivery, and in-app banners.

Module 15

Reports & Analytics

Comprehensive visual charts for cumulative mortality, FCR growth trend, daily feed intake curves, egg production history, and printable CSV summaries.

Module 16

User Profile & Roles

Farmer, Veterinarian, and Admin role preferences, profile updates, language toggle (English / Telugu), phone verification, and session security.
