Flock Health & Biosecurity Recommendations

Evidence-based preventive management protocols tailored to current microclimate and flock risk levels

AI Safety & Ethical Guidance: All protocols provided below represent standard non-therapeutic husbandry, environmental, and biosecurity preventive measures. This system does not prescribe drug dosages or antibiotics. Always contact an avian veterinarian for diagnostic necropsy and prescription treatments.
1. Environment & Microclimate Ventilation
Priority: High
Continuous Air Exchange & Ammonia Control Increase tunnel ventilation exhaust speeds during afternoon peak temperatures to maintain ammonia (NH3) concentrations safely below 15 ppm.
Evaporative Cooling Pad Inspection Check cooling pad water pumps and air filters twice daily to prevent uneven humidity pockets or cold drafts.
Optimal Litter Dryness Monitoring Maintain poultry litter moisture between 20% and 25%. Immediately rake or top-dress damp areas near drinker lines to inhibit coccidial oocyst sporulation.
2. Feed & Water Quality Management
Priority: Medium
Water Line Sanitization & Flushing Flush enclosed nipple drinker lines daily with cool, potable water. Ensure water temperature remains below 24°C to sustain voluntary consumption.
Anti-Stress Hydration & Electrolytes Provide clean water enriched with commercial poultry electrolyte stabilizers during heat-stress periods to maintain osmotic balance.
Feed Freshness & Mould Prevention Inspect feed pans and storage silos for feed caking or mould growth. Run feeders early in the morning and late in the evening to stimulate appetite.
3. Shed Hygiene & Biosecurity Barriers
Priority: High
Disinfection Footbaths & Boot Changes Enforce dedicated shed footwear and replenish disinfectant footbaths every 48 hours using approved broad-spectrum biocides.
Wild Bird & Rodent Exclusion Verify integrity of wire mesh netting across all air inlets and repair tears immediately to avoid wild bird contamination.
Vehicle & Equipment Spray Wash All feed transport trucks and visitor vehicles must pass through pressurized tyre wash bays prior to perimeter entry.
4. Health Surveillance & Isolation
Priority: Medium
Immediate Sick Bird Isolation Gently separate birds exhibiting sneezing, eye discharge, or extreme lethargy into a dedicated sick bay to curb airborne transmission.
Daily Walk-Throughs & Posture Checks Conduct morning and evening walks along side walls and drinker lines. Note bird vocalizations, huddling patterns, and feather quality.
Prompt Veterinary Consultation If daily mortality exceeds 0.2% of the flock or respiratory rales persist for >24 hours, contact your veterinarian for diagnostic post-mortem.
Need Professional Veterinary Consultation?

Contact your regional government avian health diagnostic lab or certified private poultry veterinarian for farm visits and tissue sampling.
