Introduction & Scope

Transforming Traditional Poultry into Precision Agriculture

Traditional poultry farming depends primarily on manual visual observation, periodic temperature measurements, and retrospective mortality counts. This approach often leads to delayed reactions during acute thermal stress ($>34^\circ\text{C}$), undetected toxic ammonia gas accumulation ($\ge 25\text{ ppm}$), unmeasured feed wastage, and rapid disease transmission across flock batches.

The Smart Poultry Farm Management and Monitoring System provides continuous IoT telemetry, AI disease risk classification, feed conversion ratio (FCR) growth curve analysis, automated vaccination schedules, and vernacular Telugu localization, providing a closed-loop digital solution.

Architecture

16 Integrated System Modules

Modular components covering the entire lifecycle of poultry operations.

1. Dashboard

Real-time live bird count, feed, water, eggs, mortality, and live IoT gauge.

2. Farm Management

Multi-farm setup, shed capacity limits, and biosecurity protocols.

3. Flock Management

Batches, breed selection (Cobb/Ross), bird age, and status progression.

4. Feed Management

Daily consumption logs, hopper level telemetry, and 7-day requirement prediction.

5. Water Management

Daily water intake in liters, reservoir depth telemetry, and low-tank alarms.

6. Egg Production

Collection counts, damaged egg reconciliation, and laying percentage.

7. Mortality Tracking

Casualty records, cause-of-death categorization, and spike mortality detection.

8. Weight Monitoring

Sample weighing, average weight tracking, and standard growth curves.

9. Temperature Monitoring

Continuous shed thermal telemetry with heat-stress alerts ($T \ge 34^\circ\text{C}$).

10. Humidity Monitoring

Relative humidity % tracking to control litter moisture and pathogen growth.

11. Vaccination Records

Age-based vaccine calendar, administration routes, and vet consultation.

12. Expenses and Sales

Purchase of chicks, feed, medicines, bird and egg sales with net profit.

13. Inventory

Feed bag quantities, medicine vials, and automatic re-order alerts.

14. Tasks & Notifications

Daily shed routines, vaccination reminders, and multi-channel SMS warnings.

15. Reports & Analytics

FCR performance curves, cumulative mortality trends, and CSV data export.

16. User Profile & Roles

Farmer, Veterinarian, and Admin role permissions with bilingual toggle.
